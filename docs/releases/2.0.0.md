# SumUp Terminal for WooCommerce 2.0.0

This is the 2.0 version of SumUp Terminal for WooCommerce, made for WooCommerce POS 2.0. It needs WooCommerce POS Pro 2.0.0 or newer: with an older Pro, or none, the plugin does nothing and shows an admin notice. SumUp Terminal is no longer offered on your shop's web checkout; it works only in the POS.

## Before you update

- Update WooCommerce POS Pro to 2.0.0 or newer first.
- Web checkout is removed, and the "Enable SumUp Terminal for web checkout" setting is gone. Card payments with SumUp now happen on the POS keypad and the POS order-pay page.

## New

- SumUp Solo checkout from the POS keypad, with cancel from the till and full or partial refunds from the POS.
- **POS connection** setting: **Bluetooth reader** (the POS app drives a Solo Lite, Solo or Air; needs the Affiliate Key) or **Solo over the cloud** (the store drives the reader). With nothing saved, the cloud is used, so the SumUp tile shows on every till.
- **Default terminal**, **Enabled terminals** and **Lock terminal selection** settings choose which SumUp readers cashiers can pick at checkout.
- **Offline card payments** setting (off by default): a Bluetooth Solo or Solo Lite can take card payments while the POS is offline. SumUp must enable offline payments on your account first. An offline payment can be declined when it uploads, and your business carries that risk.
- **Affiliate App ID** and **Affiliate Key** settings.

## Changed

- The POS order-pay page (Legacy tab) uses WooCommerce POS Pro's shared payment panel. Payments left in progress by the old panel are carried over on upgrade.
- The Solo pairing help names the right menu item: Connections → Cloud API, last in the list. If Cloud API is not shown, log out of your SumUp account on the reader and check again.

## Fixed

- A cancel from the till is confirmed in about three seconds instead of twelve, and a cancelled checkout on a physical Solo no longer waits five minutes to clear.
- If SumUp does not answer a checkout (a dropped connection, a timeout, an outage), the payment stays pending instead of being dropped, and trying again sends nothing more to the reader.
- When SumUp refuses a checkout, for example in a currency your account cannot take, the till shows SumUp's message and stops.
- SumUp reports a declined card and a cancel on the reader the same way, so the POS no longer calls a declined card a cancel.
- A POS refund of an order paid on the old order-pay panel finds its SumUp transaction.
- A replayed or forged result delivery can no longer mark a POS payment as paid. The delivery must carry the token of its own checkout and name a transaction of that payment. Checkouts started on a 2.0 beta have no token and settle by polling.
- A payment the old panel took could drop out of the completion queue when the webhook and the sweep ran at the same moment. It now stays queued, and SumUp is asked to deliver again if the queue is busy.
- The sweep no longer ends another sale on the same Solo when it cancels an expired payment carried over from the old panel.
- A checkout SumUp accepted with an empty or unreadable answer stays pending instead of failing, since it may be on the reader. A checkout is sent once, never again after a dropped connection or an outage.
- A refund SumUp gave no clear answer to (a dropped connection, a timeout, an outage) is no longer reported as failed, since it may have gone through. The amount stays reserved and staff are asked to check it in SumUp, so the same refund cannot run twice. A refund SumUp refuses still frees the amount for another try.
